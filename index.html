FeedTag Scan

Instantly check if a feed bag suits your horse

Scan or type the tag on a feed sack, pair it with your horse's weight, workload, and health flags, and get a clear go/no-go recommendation with vet-grade notes on ration size, NSC risk, and forage pairing.

Why it matters

Most owners eyeball nutrition. This companion keeps horses off the metabolic rollercoaster by matching feed formulation to a vetted profile instead of marketing claims.

How a scan works

  • Capture the printed feed tag or type the lot number.
  • We decode protein, fat, NSC, and feeding directions.
  • We overlay your horse's workload, weight, age, and risk flags.

What you get

  • Green/yellow/red suitability with rationale.
  • Daily pounds to feed with forage pairing guidance.
  • Auto-alerts for laminitis/PPID/ulcer-friendly selections.

Preview it right now

  • Open index.html directly or run python -m http.server 8000 and visit http://localhost:8000.
  • The sample "Balanced Maintenance Pellets" scan is already preloaded.
  • No backend needed — it is a single static page with baked-in demo data.
