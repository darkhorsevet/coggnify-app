# Askapy Logo Design Guide

## Requirements for ChatGPT Plugin

**Technical Specs:**
- **Size:** 512x512 pixels
- **Format:** PNG
- **Background:** Transparent
- **File size:** < 100KB
- **URL:** https://api.askapy.com/logo.png

---

## Design Concepts

### Option 1: Paw + Chat Bubble (RECOMMENDED)

```
     💬
    /  \
   | 🐾 |
    \__/
```

**Elements:**
- Chat bubble (represents AI/conversation)
- Paw print inside (represents pets)
- Clean, simple design
- Colors: Green (#10b981) and Blue (#3b82f6)

**Why this works:**
- Instantly recognizable
- Combines tech + pets
- Works at small sizes
- Memorable

---

### Option 2: Stethoscope + Paw

```
   🐾
   ||
  (())
   \/
```

**Elements:**
- Veterinary stethoscope
- Integrated paw print
- Professional look
- Colors: Medical green + navy

**Why this works:**
- Clearly veterinary
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Unique

---

### Option 3: Abstract Pet Shapes

```
  🐕 🐱
   \ /
   🐴 🐄
```

**Elements:**
- Simple animal silhouettes in circle
- Dog, cat, horse, cattle
- Arranged in friendly pattern
- Gradient: green to blue

**Why this works:**
- Shows all animals supported
- Friendly, approachable
- Not too medical (less intimidating)

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors:
- **Veterinary Green:** #10b981 (trust, health, growth)
- **Tech Blue:** #3b82f6 (intelligence, reliability)
- **White:** #ffffff (clean, simple)

### Gradient Option:
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #3b82f6 100%);
```

---

## Typography

**For "Askapy" wordmark:**
- **Font:** SF Pro Rounded or similar
- **Weight:** Bold (700)
- **Style:** Modern, friendly, tech-forward

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Too complex (won't scale down)
❌ Too many colors (confusing)
❌ Thin lines (won't be visible small)
❌ Text-heavy (hard to read)
❌ Generic pet store look
❌ Scary/medical (we want approachable)

## Testing Your Logo

**Before finalizing, test:**

1. **At different sizes:**
   - 512x512 (full size)
   - 128x128 (app store)
   - 64x64 (plugin store)
   - 32x32 (favicon)
   - Does it still look good?

2. **On different backgrounds:**
   - White background
   - Black background
   - ChatGPT interface
   - Mobile screens

3. **Recognition test:**
   - Show to 5 people
   - Ask: "What does this app do?"
   - If they say pets/vets = success!

## Brand Guidelines

**Once you have logo:**

### Do:
✅ Use on white or dark backgrounds
✅ Maintain clear space around logo
✅ Keep proportions intact
✅ Use approved colors

### Don't:
❌ Stretch or distort
❌ Change colors randomly
❌ Add effects or shadows
❌ Place on busy backgrounds
